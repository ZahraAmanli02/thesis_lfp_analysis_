Reading guide:
  - Estrous is not diet-related by design, so it cannot add
    diet information; adding it as a feature is not expected to
    raise accuracy.
  - lfp_residualized asks the principled question: with estrous-
    driven variance removed, does an LFP diet signal emerge? If
    it stays near chance, the RQ1 conclusion is robust to estrous.
