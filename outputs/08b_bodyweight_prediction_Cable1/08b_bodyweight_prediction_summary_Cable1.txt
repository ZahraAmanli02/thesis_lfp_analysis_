======================================================================
08B BODY-WEIGHT PREDICTION FROM LFP — Cable1
======================================================================

Observations : 125 recordings / 19 mice
Target       : body weight (mean 31.8 g, range 25.2-40.0 g)
Features     : 29 LFP-derived (weight is the target; diet group excluded)
Validation   : leave-one-mouse-out
Baseline MAE (predict the mean) : 3.01 g

----------------------------------------------------------------------
model                  R2    MAE (g)  vs baseline
----------------------------------------------------------------------
ridge              -0.151       3.19        +0.17
svr_rbf            -0.253       3.26        +0.24
random_forest      -0.371       3.51        +0.49

----------------------------------------------------------------------
PERMUTATION TEST (Ridge, LOMO)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  observed R2   : -0.151
  null-mean R2  : -0.236
  p-value (1000) : 0.2218

Reading guide:
  - R2 <= 0 means LFP does not predict weight better than just
    guessing the average weight (MAE >= baseline).
  - MAE is in grams: 'on average we are off by this much'.
  - A positive R2 that beats the permutation null would be
    evidence of a genuine LFP -> body-weight relationship.
