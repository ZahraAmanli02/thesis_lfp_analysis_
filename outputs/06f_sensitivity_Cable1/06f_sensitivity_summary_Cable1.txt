06F SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS (MANN-WHITNEY vs MIXED MODEL) — Cable1
======================================================================================

Sample:  89 recordings / 19 mice (diet-phase only)

Mann-Whitney U (two-sided) compares HF vs CTRL on raw
recordings, treating all 89 recordings as independent.
This is a LIBERAL test (it ignores mouse-level clustering),
so the comparison with the mixed-model result is the
methodological point of this script: a naive test can hide
an effect that mixed-effects modelling recovers.

Rank-biserial r: non-parametric analogue of Cohen's d.
  positive r = HF higher; negative r = HF lower; range [-1,+1].
