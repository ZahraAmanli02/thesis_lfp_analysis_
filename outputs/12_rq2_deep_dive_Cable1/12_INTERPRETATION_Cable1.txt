SCIENTIFIC READING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Weight regression from LFP alone is HARDER than diet classification.
  This asymmetry is scientifically informative: chronic HFD exposure
  leaves a categorical signature in the LFP that is easier to decode
  than the continuous body-weight dimension. This is consistent with
  the fact that body weight reflects many non-neural factors (diet
  intake, activity, hormones), while diet group is a controlled
  categorical variable.

- The best weight-predictive cell — phase A x high_gamma_beta —
  lies in the gamma frequency range, consistent with gamma's role in
  LH feeding and energy-related circuits (Carus-Cadavieco et al., 2017).

- Prediction is not equally good across phases: this validates the
  per-phase modelling approach and suggests that estrous state gates
  the strength of the weight signal in the LFP.
